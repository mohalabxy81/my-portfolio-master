About Me

Civil Site Execution & Supervision Engineer

I am a passionate Civil Engineer holding a Bachelor's Degree in Civil Engineering from Delta University for Science and Technology (Graduation Project: Highway Engineering with Grade Excellent).

My journey combines rigorous field execution with technical supervision. I served as an Execution & Supervision Engineer with the Armed Forces Engineering Authority on monumental national projects—including the City of Treasures (Marble & Granite City), the Galala City & Resort Complex, and the Dakhla – East Al Owainat Highway.

Currently, I serve as a Site Execution & Structural Engineer with ArteVo Co., leading the turnkey construction of industrial factories in the Kom Oshim Industrial Zone (Fayoum)—directing deep and shallow foundations, structural concrete framing, pre-engineered steel structures, heavy-duty power-troweled (helicopter) industrial concrete flooring, and MEP networks. Previously, I supervised luxury residential finishing and villa handovers with West Somid Development.
